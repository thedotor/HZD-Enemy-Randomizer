Horizon Zero Dawn - Enemy Randomizer: Map - development history
These are the builds made before version 1.0.0 (development build numbers 1.3 - 2.9.2),
newest first. Version 1.0.0 has the same features as development build 2.9.2.

DEV BUILD 2.9.2
 - Removed the experimental Machine size slider (added in 2.9). Tested in game: the game ignores
   the size of a spawn spot, and the engine has no size setting for machines (it only resizes
   things from scripted animations). Stretching the model itself would leave the skeleton and
   hitboxes at normal size, so it isn't worth doing.

DEV BUILD 2.8
 - Humans ⇄ machines (Humans tab, experimental): some machine spots get a human enemy instead,
   and some human spots get a machine. Two sliders (0-50%, default 10%):
     Machines → humans - small machines become archers/brawlers, medium ones any fighter,
                         Large ones heavies, flyers become snipers. In The Cut they become
                         Frozen Wilds bandits; elsewhere bandits or Eclipse.
     Humans → machines - Small or Medium machines; heavies become a Large machine only where
                         there's room. Bandit camps are left out unless you tick
                         "Bandit camps can get machines too".
   Only single spots are swapped - herds, groups, bosses and hand-picked spots never are. Quest,
   Cauldron and Hunting Ground spots only if those are ticked. Uses your switched-off machines,
   Rare/Common weights and human toughness. Has its own seed. Asks first.
   With spoilers on, a machine spot that became a person shows a small person in the faction
   colour; a person that became a machine shows that machine in a gold diamond.
   Untested in game: people may wander or stand idle at machine spots, and machines may get
   stuck in human areas. Make a manual save; untick and rebuild if anything breaks.
 - Fixed: with spoilers on, a few map markers and tooltips could show a human's change on a
   machine spot that happened to share its number.

DEV BUILD 2.7 - new layout
 - The right-hand panel is now split into four tabs:
     World    - Whole map, Areas, Area settings, Roaming herds (herd size moved here)
     Machines - Which fights (Cauldrons, story, Hunting Grounds), Bosses, Extra machines
                (Rockbreaker, Corruptor, Deathbringer), Frozen Wilds & flyers, Machine toughness,
                spots picked by hand
     Humans   - Human enemies, Human toughness, type weights
     Setup    - Game folder, Recent builds, Presets, Uninstall
   Each tab shows a short status under its name (e.g. "Whole map", "3 risky", "Swap camps").
   Wherever the README says "Options", look on the Machines tab.
 - Build & install, Preview and Surprise me (🎲) are always at the bottom of the panel.
 - Above them, orange tags list every risky option that's switched on - click one to jump to it.
 - The game-folder status sits at the top (click it to change the folder).
 - Preview opens as its own panel beside the map (× to close).
 - The map key can be folded away (Key ▸ / ▾), and the Machines list help is tucked under
   "How this list works".
 - Settings, seeds and builds are unchanged - same setup, same result.

DEV BUILD 2.6
 - Preview (Install section): counts what your setup will put in the world before you install -
   every machine, how many map spots and how many herd machines, and how much of it is Large.
   Nothing is installed. Change a setting and press Preview again.
 - Pick a spot's machine yourself: click any machine on the map and choose
     Randomize as normal - follows your areas and ticks (default)
     Keep it as it is    - this spot never changes
     a machine           - that machine goes there, whatever else is set
   Picked spots get a small gold dot (grey = kept). Hand-picked machines are placed even on spots
   whose tick is off, and they skip the room check - a big machine in a tight spot can get stuck.
   "Clear picked spots" (Options) removes them all.
 - Human weights: Human enemies > "How often each type is picked" - Rare / Normal / Common for
   each human type, like the machines.
 - The one Deathbringer that's already in the game's patrol list (part of a story fight) now
   only changes when "Randomize boss fights" is ticked.

DEV BUILD 2.5.1
 - The Deathbringer has its own map icon.

DEV BUILD 2.5
 - "Add the Corruptor to the Large group" (Options): Corruptors can appear wherever Large
   machines can, in areas and roaming herds. They corrupt the machines around them.
 - "Add the Deathbringer to the Large group" (Options, experimental): the same for
   Deathbringers. The game has no open-world Deathbringer, so this reuses the one from a
   main-quest boss fight - it may stand idle or act oddly away from its arena. Herds get at
   most one. Make a manual save first; untick and rebuild if it misbehaves.
   Both ask first, and both are off by default. Unticking puts them back in "Never randomized".

DEV BUILD 2.4
 - 357 more machine spots found and added (920 in total, was 563): corrupted zones, the machines
   around Tallnecks, more main quest, side quest and tribe quest fights, and a few world spots
   the old scan missed. They follow your areas and ticks like the others (quest fights follow the
   story-missions tick). Spots you already had keep their numbering, so a seed you liked still
   gives (almost always) the same machines there.
 - Hunting Grounds (Options): "Also randomize Hunting Grounds" swaps the machines at all the
   Hunting Grounds, including the Frozen Wilds one. Asks first: trials that need a particular
   machine may become impossible while it's on - finish the trials you care about first.
 - Bosses (Options): "Randomize boss fights" swaps 19 boss machines - Deathbringers and
   Corruptors in the main quest, the Sawtooth fight with Rost, the Frozen Wilds Frostclaws,
   the Fireclaw in Cauldron EPSILON and the Rockbreaker quest. Choose what they become:
     Big machine - another Large machine (default)
     Medium+     - any Medium or Large machine
     Anything    - anything at all
   Has its own seed. Switched-off machines are never picked. Boss spots have a dotted gold ring
   on the map. Asks first: scripted bosses (mounted guns, parts to shoot off) may not finish with
   a different machine. MAKE A MANUAL SAVE BEFORE EACH BOSS. If one gets stuck: untick, Build &
   install, load that save.

DEV BUILD 2.3
 - Human toughness (in the Human enemies section): Health and Damage sliders (25% - 300%) for
   Bandits, Eclipse and Frozen Wilds bandits. Open "Fine-tune each type" for a pair of sliders
   per enemy type (archer, heavy, priest, sniper...). They stack with the faction slider:
   Bandits Health 200% x Bandit heavy 150% = 300%.
   Works even with the human Mode set to Off, and covers every human enemy of that faction:
   camps, patrols, Eclipse ambushes, quest fights and Cauldron XI.
   Enemies that already hit harder than normal (e.g. bandit heavies) have that bonus scaled
   rather than replaced. Stealth takedowns that always kill outright may still do so.
   "Reset humans to 100%" clears all of them.

DEV BUILD 2.2
 - Per-machine HP and DMG sliders under every machine in the Machines list (left), 25% - 300%.
   They stack with the size-group sliders: Large Health 150% x Thunderjaw HP 200% = 300%.
   Hover the % to see the total. "Reset all to 100%" (Machine toughness) clears both.
   Switched-off machines don't show sliders (they're replaced anyway).

DEV BUILD 2.1
 - Machine toughness (right panel): Health and Damage sliders (25% - 300%) for each size group
   (Small, Medium, Large, Flyer, Other*). 100% = normal; they work on top of your game difficulty.
   Health 200% = machines take half damage from your arrows, bombs, fire, shock, frost hits,
   corruption, melee and Silent Strikes. Tearing parts off is not changed.
   Damage 50% = their attacks hurt you half as much.
   Applies to every machine placed in the world, including Cauldrons, story fights and bosses.
   Not applied to roaming herds and patrols (the game spawns those a different way).
   * Other = machines in no size group: Corruptor, Deathbringer, Tallneck (and the Rockbreaker
     unless you added it to Large).
   Uses the same damage modifiers the game already puts on its toughest human enemies.

DEV BUILD 2.0
 - Human enemies: a new "Human enemies" section randomizes bandits, Eclipse and Frozen Wilds
   bandits (570 spawns: bandit camps, Eclipse sites, patrols, Cauldron XI, quest fights).
   Friendly people (villagers, hunters, traders) are never touched.
     Same faction - each human becomes another type of their own faction (a bandit archer might
                    become a bandit heavy). Camps always stay on one side. Safest.
     Swap camps   - each encounter switches to another faction (an Eclipse site becomes bandits
                    and so on); archers stay archers, heavies stay heavies.
     Chaos        - any type anywhere (asks first): factions get mixed and may fight each other.
   "Also swap the faction of bandit camps" (asks first): bandit camps are activities, and a camp
   full of Eclipse might not count as cleared - without the tick, camps only get mixed bandit types.
   "Frozen Wilds bandits can appear outside The Cut": lets the tougher FW bandits turn up anywhere.
   Quest and Cauldron fights follow the ticks under Options. Areas and Whole map don't change humans.
 - "Humans" button in the top bar shows human enemies on the map as diamonds
   (orange = bandits, purple = Eclipse, blue = Frozen Wilds bandits).

DEV BUILD 1.9
 - Roaming encounters: Shell-Walker convoys (with their Watcher / Longleg / Behemoth escorts) and
   random machine encounters (Glinthawk ambushes, the alarmed Longleg, Eclipse fleeing machines,
   Glinthawk flocks) are now randomized too - 25 more spawns. They move or appear in random
   places, so they aren't shown on the map; they follow the "Roaming herds & patrols" setting
   (or the Whole map setting when it includes herds).
 - Rockbreaker option (Options, asks first): adds the Rockbreaker to the Large group so it can
   appear wherever Thunderjaws, Behemoths, Frostclaws and Fireclaws can. Outside its quarries it
   may struggle to burrow - untick and rebuild if it causes trouble.
 - Note: the Frozen Wilds has no separate roaming herds - its machines are all at fixed spots,
   which the map already covers.

DEV BUILD 1.8.1
 - Fix: big machines spawning inside each other. Many spots spawn a small group close together
   (e.g. "3 Watchers within 10 m"). When a bigger machine takes over such a group it now spawns
   fewer of them: 1 for Large machines, up to 2 for flyers, up to 3 for Medium replacing Small.
 - Single spots only get a Large machine (Thunderjaw, Behemoth, Frostclaw, Fireclaw) if no other
   spot of that encounter is within 12 m and no other big machine is within 30 m; otherwise a
   smaller machine is picked there instead.
 - Roaming herds: a Large machine taking over a herd member now comes as 1 at normal herd size.

DEV BUILD 1.8
 - Herd size (Options): make roaming herds and patrols smaller or bigger, from 25% to 300%.
   It works on its own (herd mode Off) or together with randomized herds.
 - Safer mixed herds: when a bigger machine takes over a herd member of smaller machines, its
   count is capped (at normal size: up to 6 medium, 1 large or 3 flyers; the caps grow with the
   herd size). Before, Chaotic herds could turn 20 Grazers into 20 of anything.
 - Big herds put many more machines on screen; above 150% the page shows a warning.

DEV BUILD 1.7
 - "Surprise me" (Install box): picks a random mode and seed for the whole map and roaming herds,
   then builds and installs straight away. Spoilers stay hidden.
 - Rare / Normal / Common (Machines list, left): how often each machine is picked when spots are
   swapped. Click the button to cycle it. Common is about 8x as likely as Rare. With everything on
   Normal you get exactly the same layouts as before for the same seed.
 - Find a machine (top bar): type a name (e.g. "Sawtooth") to highlight its spots and fade the
   rest; it also shows how many spots it has. Esc or the x clears it.
 - Presets (right panel): save your current setup under a name, load it later, or delete it.
   "Copy code" copies a short code (starts with HZD1-) that you can send to someone; paste a
   code into the box and press "Load code" to use it. Presets don't include your game folder.
 - Recent builds (Install box): your last 10 builds with time, mode and how many spots changed.
   "Restore" puts that setup back; press Build & install to get the same layout again.
 - Fixed: a small coloured dot was drawn over some machine icons on the map.

DEV BUILD 1.6.1
 - The Machines list has moved to its own column on the left-hand side of the page.

DEV BUILD 1.6
 - "Machines" list (left-hand side of the page): every machine with an on/off switch.
   Switch one off to keep it out of your game - every map spot and roaming-herd member that
   would be that machine gets a different machine of a similar size instead (another size if the
   whole group is off), and nothing is ever turned into it. This works even where an area's
   Mode is Off. "All on" switches everything back on.
   Their spots on the map get a red slash.
   Limits: spots inside Cauldrons and story missions are only replaced if those are ticked under
   Options; Hunting Grounds trials, bosses and scripted quest spawns are never changed.

DEV BUILD 1.5
 - Freeze Bellowback is now its own machine (Medium group): its spots are randomized and it can
   turn up anywhere a Medium machine can. Codename: spraybot_cryo.
 - New "Corrupted / Daemonic" slider (in each area's settings and in Whole map): the chance that
   a normal spot gets the corrupted version of whatever machine ends up there - or the Daemonic
   version inside The Cut. 0% (default) keeps everything as it was. Machines without such a
   version (e.g. Frostclaw has no corrupted version) stay normal. Corrupted/Daemonic machines may
   fight nearby normal machines. Roaming herds always use normal versions.
 - With "Show spoilers" on, the list and tooltips say "(corrupted)" or "(Daemonic)" where that
   version was placed.

DEV BUILD 1.4.6
 - Spoilers are hidden: after Build & install you only see how many spots changed. The list of
   what became what, the gold rings on changed markers and the "→ new machine" in tooltips
   stay hidden until you press "Show spoilers" (press again to hide them).

DEV BUILD 1.4.5
 - "Whole map" box at the top of the panel: tick "Randomize the whole map at once" to use one
   setting (Grouped/Chaotic, seed, swap chance, difficulty) for every machine on the map.
   Tick "Include roaming herds & patrols" to give herds the same mode and seed too.
   Your areas are kept but paused (greyed out) while it is ticked - untick it to go back to them.
   Cauldrons, story missions and Frozen Wilds machines still follow the ticks under Options.

DEV BUILD 1.4.4
 - Fixes a Windows Defender false alarm (Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.B!ml). The folder picker and
   "open the browser" step now use Windows' own built-in functions instead of starting a hidden
   PowerShell window, and the program now carries proper version/description details.

DEV BUILD 1.4.3
 - Click a name to switch it on and off: region names (on the map or the buttons in the Areas
   panel) and area names on the map. First click on a region makes it an area (on); after that
   each click switches it off / on.  ● = on (filled in the area's colour), ○ = off (dashed).
   Clicking a name in the Areas list still opens that area's settings - use its switch there.

DEV BUILD 1.4.2
 - On/off switch next to every area in the Areas list (and Rest of the world).
   Off = the area is ignored but kept with all its settings, so you can switch it back on later.
   Spots inside a switched-off area follow whatever is underneath it (another area, or Rest of
   the world). Switching off Rest of the world leaves everything outside your areas unchanged.
   Switched-off areas show dashed and faded on the map, with "(off)" after the name.
   Remember to press Build & install after switching areas on or off.

DEV BUILD 1.4.1
 - Game folder can be changed any time: the "Game folder" box at the top of the panel shows
   where the game is (Found / Not found). Press Change, then Browse... (pick the folder in a
   normal Windows window), "Find it for me" (searches your Steam libraries), or paste a path.
   You can choose the Horizon Zero Dawn folder or its Packed_DX12 folder.
 - Moved to another PC or drive? If the saved folder isn't there, the program first searches for
   the game, and if it can't find it the box opens and asks where it is now.

DEV BUILD 1.4
 - Mixed herds: with "Roaming herds & patrols" on, every herd member is rolled on its own,
   so herds and patrols come out as a mix of machines instead of one type becoming another.
 - Ready-made regions: dashed boxes for the big map regions (The Embrace, Nora Sacred Land,
   Carja Sundom, Meridian & the Mesa, The Jewel & the south, Sunfall & the west,
   The Claim & Free Heap, Banuk lands, The Cut). Click a region's label on the map, or its
   button in the Areas panel, to turn it into an area. "Regions" in the top bar hides the boxes.
 - Frozen Wilds machines anywhere: two new tick boxes let Scorchers, Frostclaws and Fireclaws
   appear outside The Cut - one for Grouped areas (and Grouped herds), one for Chaotic.
   Unticked (the default), they only appear in The Cut.

DEV BUILD 1.3
 - Frozen Wilds (The Cut): its machine sites are on the map and can be randomized,
   including Scorchers, Frostclaws and Fireclaws.
 - Cauldron entrances are on the map (SIGMA, RHO, XI, ZETA and EPSILON). Hover one to see
   how many machine spawns are inside.
 - Ticking "Also randomize Cauldrons" or "Also randomize story-mission fights" now shows
   a warning first, and a reminder stays visible while it is on.
