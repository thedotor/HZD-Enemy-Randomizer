# Privacy

HZD Enemy Randomizer (Map) does not collect, store or send any personal information.

- It does not connect to the internet. The map page is served only on your own PC
  (`127.0.0.1`) and opened in your web browser.
- It reads the Horizon Zero Dawn game folder you choose, and writes only:
  - `Patch_EnemyRandomizer_Map.bin` in the game's `Packed_DX12` folder (removed by *Uninstall*),
  - `EnemyRandomizer_Map_settings.json` and `EnemyRandomizer_gamedata.cache` next to the program.
- There is no telemetry, no update check and no account.

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically
requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.
