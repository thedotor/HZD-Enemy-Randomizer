Machines

How this list works
HP (health) and DMG (damage) under each machine (100% = normal) stack with the size-group sliders under Machine toughness. Rare / Normal / Common: how often a machine is picked when spots are swapped. Switch a machine off to keep it out of your game: every spot and herd member that would be that machine gets a different machine of a similar size instead, and nothing turns into it. The number is how many spots it has on the map. Click a machine on the map to pick what goes in that spot.
Enemy Randomizer

Whole map

One setting for every machine on the map. Your areas are kept but paused while this is ticked.
Corrupted / Daemonic: chance that a normal spot gets the corrupted version of its machine (the Daemonic version in The Cut). They may fight nearby normal machines. Cauldrons, story missions and Frozen Wilds machines follow the ticks on the Machines tab.
Paused - "Randomize the whole map at once" is ticked.

Areas

Ready-made regions: click a name (here or on the map) to switch it on, click again to switch it off.
Draw a box on the map (▭ Draw area, or Shift+drag).

Area settings

Mode and seed come from Whole map (herd size still applies).

Roaming herds & patrols

Wandering herds and patrol groups are shared across the whole world, so they get one setting. Every herd member gets its own roll, so a herd can come out as a mix of machines. This also covers Shell-Walker convoys and random machine encounters (Glinthawk ambushes, alarmed Longlegs and the like).
Bigger or smaller roaming herds and patrols. When a bigger machine takes over a herd of smaller ones, its numbers are capped (at normal size: up to 6 medium, 1 large or 3 flyers).
Big herds mean many more machines at once - fights get much harder and the game may slow down. Try 150% before going higher.

Which fights

Open-world spots are always included. These add riskier places - each asks first.
On: machines inside Cauldrons will be swapped. Some Cauldron fights are scripted (doors, lifts, the core override) and may not finish. Untick and rebuild if you get stuck.
On: story-mission machines will be swapped. A quest can stall if it waits for a specific machine to die or be overridden. Untick and rebuild if you get stuck.
On: Hunting Ground machines will be swapped. Trials that ask for a particular machine (e.g. knock the horns off Lancehorns) may become impossible - finish the trials you care about first. Untick and rebuild to put them back.

Bosses

Deathbringers and Corruptors in main quests, the Sawtooth fight with Rost, the Frozen Wilds Frostclaws, the Fireclaw in Cauldron EPSILON and the Rockbreaker quest. Uses its own setting, not the areas.
On: boss fights use other machines. A boss quest can stall if it waits for that exact machine (a gun to man, a part to shoot off). Make a manual save before each one; untick and rebuild if you get stuck.

Extra machines

Machines normally kept out. Each joins the Large group when ticked.
On: Rockbreakers can appear wherever Large machines can. Outside their quarries they may struggle to burrow.
On: Corruptors can appear wherever Large machines can. They corrupt other machines nearby.
On (experimental): Deathbringers can appear wherever Large machines can. It's the main-quest Deathbringer - if one stands idle or acts oddly, untick and rebuild.

Frozen Wilds & flyers

Frozen Wilds machines (Scorcher, Frostclaw, Fireclaw) outside The Cut:
Unticked, they only appear in The Cut.

Machine toughness

Health and damage for each size group, on top of your game difficulty. 100% = normal. Works on every machine placed on the map, including Cauldrons, story fights and bosses. Roaming herds are not affected (the game spawns those a different way). Fine-tune single machines with the HP / DMG sliders in the Machines list on the left.

Spots picked by hand

Human enemies

Bandits, Eclipse and Frozen Wilds bandits. One setting for the whole world (areas and Whole map don't change humans). Friendly people are never touched.
Quest and Cauldron fights follow the ticks on the Machines tab.

Humans ⇄ machines (experimental)

Some machine spots get a bandit or Eclipse fighter instead, and some human spots get a machine. Single spots only - herds, groups and bosses are never swapped. Quest, Cauldron and Hunting Ground spots only if those are ticked.
Big machines become heavies; heavies become a Large machine only where there's room. In The Cut, machines become Frozen Wilds bandits. Uses your switched-off machines and Rare/Common weights.
On (experimental): bandits at machine spots and machines at human spots may wander, stand idle or ignore you, since those places weren't built for them. Make a manual save; untick and rebuild if anything breaks.

Human toughness

Health and damage per faction, on top of your game difficulty. 100% = normal. Works even with the mode set to Off, and covers every human enemy of that faction (camps, quests, Cauldrons, Eclipse ambushes).
Fine-tune each type
Multiplies with the faction slider above.
How often each type is picked
Rare / Normal / Common, like the machines. Only matters when humans are randomized.
